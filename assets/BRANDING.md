---

## You MAY without permission

- **Reference OpenPen using the logo** in editorial or educational
  context (blog posts, comparison articles, conference slides).
- **Embed the wordmark in plugin documentation** showing OpenPen
  integration ("works with OpenPen" badges, screenshots, tutorials).
- **Take screenshots showing OpenPen UI / branding** for legitimate
  purposes (bug reports, reviews, tutorials, social-media demos).
- **Link to OpenPen's official assets** from your own materials, as
  long as the link clearly attributes them to the OpenPen project.

## You MUST NOT without permission

- **Use OpenPen branding to suggest official endorsement** of your
  plugin, product, fork, or service. Plugin names containing
  `Official`, `Verified`, or implying endorsement require written
  permission.
- **Modify the logo or wordmark to create a derivative mark.** Color
  changes, recolouring, distortion, or addition of new elements are
  not permitted on the trademark itself.
- **Use OpenPen branding on a forked or modified host distribution.**
  Forks must use a clearly distinct name, logo, and branding —
  reusing OpenPen identity would mislead users.
- **Use OpenPen branding on merchandise**, promotional items, or for
  commercial promotion outside the scope of plugin distribution.
- **Squat the OpenPen name** on social media, npm scopes, GitHub
  organisations, domain names, or app marketplaces.

## Tray icons

`tray-icon.png` and `tray-icon@2x.png` are also trademark-protected.
Plugin documentation may include screenshots showing the tray icon in
context (e.g. "after install, OpenPen appears in your menubar"); this
is fair use.

## Plugin author conventions

If you publish an OpenPen plugin, the recommended naming pattern is
`<your-name>-for-openpen` or `openpen-plugin-<feature>`. Avoid using
`OpenPen` as the primary identifier of your plugin (e.g.
`openpen-tools` is misleading). See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md)
for the full plugin naming guidelines.
